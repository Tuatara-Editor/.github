# Code of conduct

Tuatara's issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews are places to make
the editor better together. Everyone taking part, maintainers first, is
expected to:

- be considerate and patient, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and give reasons when
  declining one;
- keep to the subject of the issue or pull request at hand.

Not accepted, anywhere in the project:

- harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's
  background, identity, body or abilities;
- publishing anyone's private information without their consent;
- sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind;
- repeatedly disrupting discussion, or arguing past a maintainer's decision.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainers privately: in the
repository concerned, Security, then "Report a vulnerability", with
"conduct" in its title. Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept
confidential.

## What happens

The maintainers decide what is done: a reminder, an edited or hidden
comment, a closed issue or pull request, a temporary block, or a permanent
ban from the organisation. Approved contributors who break this code may
have their approval withdrawn.
